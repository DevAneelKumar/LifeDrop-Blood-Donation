Your Guide to Blood Donation

Simple information to help you prepare for a donation.

THREE SIMPLE STAGES

What to Expect

Follow the guidance of trained staff at an authorized donation center.

01

Before Donation

Eat regular healthy meals, drink water, sleep well, and bring identification if required.

02

During Donation

Answer health screening questions honestly and follow the donation staff's instructions.

03

After Donation

Rest, have refreshments, drink fluids, and follow the care instructions provided by staff.

DONOR WELLNESS

Is Blood Donation Right for You?

Eligibility depends on your age, health, medications, travel history, hemoglobin level, and local donation rules.

  • Speak honestly during health screening
  • Ask staff if you have any health concerns
  • Follow the recommended recovery guidance
  • Never donate if staff advise against it
Only qualified donation staff can determine your eligibility. Requirements vary by country and donation service.
