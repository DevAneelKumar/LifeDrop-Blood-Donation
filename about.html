WHO WE ARE

Driven by Compassion, United by Hope.

LifeDrop is a student frontend project designed to promote awareness about voluntary blood donation and the importance of helping others.

Our goal is to present information in a simple, friendly way so visitors can learn about blood groups, donor eligibility, and safe donation practices.

  • Promote voluntary donation awareness
  • Share basic educational resources
  • Encourage kindness and community support
