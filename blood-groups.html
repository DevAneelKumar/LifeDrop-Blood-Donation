KNOW YOUR TYPE

Eight Blood Types

Blood type is determined by specific antigens and the Rh factor.

A+

A Positive

A antigen, Rh positive

A−

A Negative

A antigen, Rh negative

B+

B Positive

B antigen, Rh positive

B−

B Negative

B antigen, Rh negative

AB+

AB Positive

A and B antigens, Rh positive

AB−

AB Negative

A and B antigens, Rh negative

O+

O Positive

Neither A nor B, Rh positive

O−

O Negative

Neither A nor B, Rh negative

WHY IT MATTERS

Blood Compatibility Is Important

Matching blood types helps reduce the risk of serious transfusion reactions. Hospitals perform blood typing and compatibility testing before transfusions.

O negative red blood cells are often used in emergencies when a patient's blood type is unknown, while AB positive recipients can generally receive red blood cells from all ABO/Rh groups. Clinical teams must still follow transfusion protocols.
